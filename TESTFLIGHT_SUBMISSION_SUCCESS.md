## What Happens Next

### Step 1: Build Processing (10-30 minutes)

Apple App Store Connect is now processing your build:

1. **Upload Complete** ✅ (already done)
2. **Processing** ⏳ (in progress - 10-30 minutes)
   - Status: "Processing"
   - Apple validates the build
   - Checks for compliance issues
3. **Ready to Test** ✅ (when processing completes)
   - Status: "Ready to Test"
   - Build appears in TestFlight tab
   - Available for adding testers

### Step 2: Add Internal Testers (Instant)

Once the build shows "Ready to Test":

1. Go to [TestFlight in App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6757497389/testflight/ios)
2. Click **"Internal Testing"** in the left sidebar
3. Click **"+"** to create a test group (e.g., "Internal Testers")
4. Select your processed build (version 1.0.0, build 1)
5. Click **"Add Testers"**
6. Enter Apple ID emails of team members
7. They'll receive an invitation email

**Internal Testing Features:**
- ✅ Up to 100 testers
- ✅ Instant access (no Beta App Review)
- ✅ Perfect for your development team
- ✅ No wait time

### Step 3: Add External Testers (Optional - Requires Review)

For beta testers outside your team:

1. Go to **TestFlight** → **"External Testing"**
2. Click **"+"** to create a test group (e.g., "Beta Testers")
3. Select your processed build
4. Add test information:
   - **What to Test:** Brief description of what testers should focus on
   - **Feedback Email:** Where to send feedback
   - **Demo Account:** If login required, provide test credentials
   - **Notes:** Any additional information for reviewers
5. Click **"Submit for Review"**
6. Wait for Beta App Review (24-48 hours typically)

**External Testing Features:**
- ✅ Up to 10,000 testers
- ⏳ Requires Beta App Review (24-48 hours)
- ✅ Perfect for larger beta testing
- ✅ Public link available (after approval)
