## Getting Started

1. **Accept the Invitation**
   - Check your email for a TestFlight invitation from Apple
   - Click "View in TestFlight" or "Start Testing"

2. **Install TestFlight** (if you don't have it)
   - Download TestFlight from the App Store
   - It's free and required to test beta apps

3. **Install Ollie**
   - Open the TestFlight app
   - Find "Ollie" in your list of apps
   - Tap "Install"

4. **Start Testing!**
   - Open Ollie and create an account
   - Explore the features
   - Test the functionality
